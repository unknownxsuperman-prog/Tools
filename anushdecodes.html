Hi, I'm Anush

Anush Decodes started as a simple idea: the things I was learning as a student and figuring out about my own health didn't need to stay in my notes app. If a guide, a study resource, or an experience could save someone else time or worry, it belonged somewhere they could actually find it.

That's what this platform is — a running collection of study material, health information shared responsibly, practical tools, and honest personal experience, kept in one place and made easy to search.

About Me

Why Anush Decodes exists, and what it's trying to do.

Anush Decodes is built around a simple belief: useful information should be easy to find, not locked behind confusing formats or scattered across a dozen places. It brings together student resources, health information, practical tools, and personal experience in one place that's meant to be revisited, not just visited once.

Free resources exist here because access shouldn't depend on who you know or what you can afford. Education, health, and practical guidance are areas where good information genuinely changes outcomes — that's the reason this keeps getting built out.
